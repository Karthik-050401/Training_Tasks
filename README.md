# Training_Tasks
This is a repo to display my understanding on the given concepts in the repo.
The repo contains executable codes, but most importantly, it has wiki pages about each concept.
The wiki page is about my personal understanding on the concepts. 
It is not documentation, but rather a mix of reference and study notes. 

I have also included my personal view on the concepts through analogies to help me picture these concepts clearly.

The seed of my analogy starts here:
Backend developers are the small builders of the huge world called software world. 
They are the architect, builder, plumber, electrician, maintenance person, organiser and lot more.
The buildings of this world are computers and the connection, let it be postal connection, water connection, food connection, are all the dependencies we have between each other.
This world has houses, apartments, offices, warehouses, roads, pipes, etc.
We as backend developer are here to understand the functioning of this world, how everything works and connects together and start building.

In each wiki page, I will add my own idea of the analogy as a section called "Analogy".
